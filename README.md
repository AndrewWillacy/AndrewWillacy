# How I Approach Analytics

My analytical approach goes beyond identifying **what** is happening. I investigate **why** it is happening, validate whether the evidence supports the conclusion and translate the findings into practical action.

My operational background taught me that data does not exist in isolation. It represents real customers, processes, decisions and consequences.

That experience now informs how I approach analytical work:

- **What is happening?**
- **Why is it happening?**
- **Can the data and assumptions be trusted?**
- **What does it mean for the business or customer?**
- **What action should follow?**

This combination of analytical capability and practical business understanding has enabled me to reduce reconciliation discrepancies by **60%**, improve operational efficiency by **40%** and reduce waste by **25%**.
